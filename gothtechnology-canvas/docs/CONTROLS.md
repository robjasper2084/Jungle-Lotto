# Controls

## Player 1

- Move: `A` / `D`
- Jump: `W`
- Crouch: `S`
- Dash: double-tap direction or `Left Shift`
- Light punch: `J`
- Heavy punch: `U`
- Light kick: `K`
- Heavy kick: `I`
- Special: `L`
- Super/MAX: `O`
- Throw: `H`
- Assist 1: `N`
- Assist 2: `M`
- Taunt: `Y`

## Player 2

- Move: arrow keys
- Dash: double-tap direction or `Right Shift`
- Light punch: `Numpad 1`
- Heavy punch: `Numpad 4`
- Light kick: `Numpad 2`
- Heavy kick: `Numpad 5`
- Special: `Numpad 3`
- Super/MAX: `Numpad 6`
- Throw: `Numpad 0`
- Assist 1: `Numpad 7`
- Assist 2: `Numpad 8`
- Taunt: `Numpad 9`

## Global

- Confirm: `Enter` or `Space`
- Pause: `P`
- CPU toggle: `C`
- Training toggle: `T`
- Debug hitboxes: `B`
- Mute: `G`
- Reset match: `R`
- Back: `Escape`

Touch controls are available on coarse-pointer screens.
