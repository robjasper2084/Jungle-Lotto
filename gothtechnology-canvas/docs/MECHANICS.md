# Mechanics

GOTHTECHNOLOGY is a side-view gothic arcade fighter. KALYX is a fast rushdown character with claw pressure, fire-slash visuals, shadow projectiles, and raven/wraith assists. MASTER EZRA is slower and controls space with blue magic, owl arcs, a stronger shield visual, and owl support.

## Match Rules

- Best of three rounds.
- 99-second round timer in arcade mode.
- KO ends the round.
- Timer loss goes to the fighter with more health.
- Training mode keeps fighters active and slowly restores health and meter.

## Combat Rules

- Attacks have startup, active, and recovery windows.
- Hits apply damage, hitstun, knockback, meter gain, and hitstop.
- Blocking reduces damage to chip and applies blockstun.
- Low attacks require low block.
- High attacks require standing block.
- Throws bypass block but require close range.
- Combo scaling reduces repeated hit damage.
- Super moves consume full MAX meter.
- Specials and assists use cooldowns.

## Implemented Systems

- Walking, running after sustained movement, and dashing.
- Jumping, air attack, landing motion support.
- Crouching, crouch attack, high block, low block.
- Light/heavy punches and kicks.
- Combo attacks, throws, specials, supers.
- Projectiles, assist strikes, hit sparks, block shields, dust/smoke-style effects.
- Hitboxes, hurtboxes, debug overlay, motion-name readout.
