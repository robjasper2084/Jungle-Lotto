# Move List

## KALYX

- Light Punch: fast claw check.
- Heavy Punch: slower heavy claw hit with stronger knockback.
- Light Kick: quick low pressure.
- Heavy Kick: long low knockback.
- Crouch Attack: low claw poke.
- Air Attack: airborne slash.
- Combo 1: multi-hit rush chain.
- Combo 2: heavier finisher chain.
- Throw: close grab into knockdown.
- Fire Slash / Shadow Claw: projectile special.
- Shadow Roar: MAX super projectile burst.
- Shadow Raven Assist: fast aerial strike.
- Nocturna Wraith Assist: slower midline pressure.

## MASTER EZRA

- Light Punch: staff-hand check.
- Heavy Punch: heavy blue-magic strike.
- Light Kick: low spacing tool.
- Heavy Kick: slower control hit.
- Crouch Attack: low staff sweep.
- Air Attack: falling magic hit.
- Combo 1: control string.
- Combo 2: heavier magic string.
- Throw: close-range control grab.
- Blue Magic: zoning projectile.
- Sky Judgment: MAX super projectile.
- Owl Companion Assist: dive strike.
- Blue Ward Assist: short shield pulse.
