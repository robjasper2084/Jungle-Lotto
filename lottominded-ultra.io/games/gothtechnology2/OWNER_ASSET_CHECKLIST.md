# Owner asset checklist — Store conversion v2

Existing supplied photos, concept scenes, videos, and originals are preserved. The media manifest records web derivatives and their sources. These files are visual references, not proof of manufacture, bundle contents, customer use, or commercial rights.

## Apparel: each offered item and color

- [ ] Approved front, back, side, and interior photographs.
- [ ] Embroidery, cuffs, hood, pocket, seams, and fabric texture.
- [ ] On-body fit, actual model height/measurements, and worn size.
- [ ] Measured size chart and size comparison.
- [ ] Physical sample and owner approval tied to the exact product/variant.

## Accessories and collectibles

- [ ] Approved front, back, scale reference, and close details.
- [ ] Attachment hardware, material/finish details, and packaging.
- [ ] Exact dimensions and confirmed included items.
- [ ] For the ashtray: approved physical sample, material, dimensions, care, and suitability information; artwork alone establishes none of these facts.

## Digital items and bundles

- [ ] Actual file contents, formats, resolutions, previews, delivery method, and license.
- [ ] Confirm precisely which products/accessories are included; styled bags and clothing are not automatically included.
- [ ] Review refund limitations and any applicable consent requirements with the owner.

## Campaign films and people

- [ ] Confirm commercial rights to each source video, image, music track, voice, likeness, logo, and derivative.
- [ ] Supply reviewed speech transcripts and synchronized captions. Adjacent visual descriptions are present but are not substitutes for speech captions.
- [ ] Confirm the 15-second commercial edit and approved soundtrack.
- [ ] Review crops on small phones and large screens.

## Media labels

Use SUPPLIED PRODUCT REFERENCE, CAMPAIGN CONCEPT, DETAIL REFERENCE, SCALE REFERENCE, or 2.5D DISPLAY as appropriate. VERIFIED PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHY requires explicit owner approval of every image covered by the product's photographyStatus. Do not approve a mixed concept/final gallery as wholly verified.

## Optional future models

The existing uploaded-a/b GLB and provenance files are retained, but catalog/hero model mappings remain null. Before binding them, verify identity, bounds, materials, mobile interaction, keyboard controls, context loss, disposal, size budget, and actual product accuracy. A tilted image is a 2.5D display, not a rotatable garment model.

## Responsive derivative change

Only two smaller versions of existing armory-hero-v2.webp were added: 640 and 1600 pixels. The existing 960 source remains available. The original source is 2048 pixels wide; no artificial 2400-pixel upscale or new campaign artwork was generated.
