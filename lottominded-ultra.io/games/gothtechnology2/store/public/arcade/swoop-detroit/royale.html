    Settings Camera, controls & graphics
    Riding camera

    Getting off switches to your on-foot view. V / View changes it; remounting restores your riding camera.

    U toggle Weapon / Brawl while on foot · in Brawl: click/Jab light attack, R/Kick heavy attack, L/Evade sidestep (steer) or back-dash (brake) · remount requires health above 15 · W/S ride / brake · A/D steer · mouse aim · left click fire · right click tap ADS, hold shoulder aim · R reload · K recover · Q/E lean while riding or walking · Alt free look · C crouch / stand · X lie down / stand · Z send dog · N dog radar · B fire mode · 1/2 or mouse wheel select · F pick up · G shield · H repair · V camera · T scopes · Tab/Escape menu. Ride or walk over a scope to collect it; switch between collected scopes and iron sights.

    Gamepad: left stick rides, right stick aims, RT fire, LT ADS, X reload, A hop, LB burst, RB shield, Y repair, D-pad up switches scopes. Touch: left stick rides; drag the right side to aim. Right Fire also aims while held. ADS toggles sights; hold Look to look around, tap Lean L/R to peek and tap again to centre, tap Crouch to duck, and Lie down to crawl (get off the wheel first). Tap again to stand. Find a gold dog whistle to charge your companion. Aim toward a nearby opponent and tap Dog / Z; Radar / N scans briefly. The dog can knock a rider off the wheel; shield blocks contact. Controls can be moved and resized. Large buttons fit automatically on small screens.
