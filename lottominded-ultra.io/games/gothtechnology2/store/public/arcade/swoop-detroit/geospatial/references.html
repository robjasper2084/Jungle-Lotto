Dequindre Cut
Environment references.

Reference index for the editable Blender level and the playable local game. Reviewed 14 September 2026.

Coordinate and elevation sources

  • OpenStreetMap around Gratiot: connected trail and ramp vertices, road bearings, bridge polygons and surrounding building footprints. Offline source extracts are in src/detroit/city-data.json and src/detroit/cut-geospatial.json in the project source.
  • City of Detroit Phase II engineering drawings, 2013: D-G0.4/5 typical cross sections and D-C6.1/3/5/6 grading sheets. These are historical design levels in City of Detroit datum feet, not WGS84 heights or a current as-built survey.
  • Detroit Riverfront Conservancy: below-grade route, access points, paving, public art and Freight Yard context.

The game origin is the mapped trail center beneath Gratiot: 42.3472034, −83.0358912. Its floor is local Y = 0, using 120.55 ft from the historical design profile. More displayed decimal places do not imply surveyed accuracy.

Google visual reference index

Google Maps place and photo gallery was inspected. A Chestnut Street panorama dated August 2021 showed dense tree banks, grassy verges, a divided paved trail, and weathered concrete parapets with graffiti. Those observations informed material and vegetation choices. The requested Gratiot panorama resolved to 1900 Gratiot Avenue but displayed a black image, so its surface details were not validated.

Satellite overlay workflow

  1. Download dequindre-map-audit.kml.
  2. Open Google Earth, then Local KML editor → Open KML/Z from device.
  3. Use north-up, top-down imagery. Inspect gold trail, orange decks, cyan ramps, green building footprints; estimated walls are available in the separate SVG audit.
  4. Record residual offsets and imagery dates before altering the coordinate anchor. Correct individual uncertain features first.

The local KML was successfully opened over Google Earth satellite imagery for the Gratiot crossing and ramp. Numeric residual measurements and individual building-edge registration remain unverified. No claims of sub-metre agreement are made.
