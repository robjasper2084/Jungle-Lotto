Gamepad: left stick / triggers to ride, A to hop, LB to crouch, B for the selected trick, RB to choose a trick, Y for camera, X to recover.

Special moves: Start upright on the ground. Curb hop: below 25 km/h. Spins: below 14 km/h; a 180 lands riding backward. One-foot glide: 4–18 km/h with gentle steering. Each move preloads automatically. Steer left or right when starting a spin to choose its direction. Settle for 0.6 seconds to validate a move. Pending combo points bank after 5 quiet seconds or a valid finish. Collisions and hard or incomplete landings cancel the award.

New flow moves: 5 rolling pirouette (below 5 km/h), 6 pendulum (below 4 km/h), 7 tuck hop (below 22 km/h). Choose these in the Trick menu on touch screens. Spins and pendulums finish on the ground; tuck hops need a clean landing.
One-foot stop: Brake to a standstill and human riders plant a boot; mascots keep both feet on the pedals. Accelerate to lift it back onto the pedal. On touch screens, use the Brake pad or pull the stick down.
Wheel feedback: Turn Sound on for warning beeps and pedal scrape. Beeps speed up above 61 km/h; ease off before tiltback. Hard full-lock carves above 36 km/h can scrape a pedal, slow the wheel and throw sparks on paving. Release the turn to clear the scrape. The warning also flashes with sound off.

Give walkers and cyclists room. Ease off when the wheel warns; landing sideways or hitting obstacles can throw the rider.
