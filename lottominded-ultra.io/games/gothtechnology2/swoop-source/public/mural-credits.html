The art along the Cut

The mural photographs in this local game come from the Detroit Riverfront Conservancy’s public mural gallery. Section locations follow its 2025 Art Walk map. Game wall dimensions, texture fitting and positions are approximations, not a survey or a complete current mural inventory.

  • Ivan Montoya, 2022 — Adelaide Street, wall and underside.
  • Sydney James, 2018 — Wilkins Street, wall and underside.
  • Freddy Diaz, 2019 — Division Street.
  • Fel3000ft, 2012 — Antietam Avenue section.
  • Mitchell Schorr, 2014 — Larned Street, “Da Race.”
  • Hygienic Dress League, 2014 — between Larned and Lafayette. Reference photograph credited in its source filename to Pravin Sitaraman.
  • Mike Han / community, We Are Detroit, 2013 — Antietam Avenue. User-supplied historic photograph; the small original limits sharpness.
  • Blue dragon mural — user-supplied photograph, also present in the Conservancy gallery as pravinsitaraman-dequindrecutart1.jpg. Chestnut placement is provisional; exact wall and artist are not verified.

Original artwork and photographs remain the work of their respective creators. Source availability and credit do not establish a redistribution license. No endorsement by the artists or Conservancy is implied.

The Detroit heart field sign and Adelaide retaining-wall graphic use artwork supplied by the game owner. Those are authored game additions.
