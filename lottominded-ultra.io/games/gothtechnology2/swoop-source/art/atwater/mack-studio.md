# 2000 Mack Avenue / GothTech Studio

The requested Maceri Produce / Pellerito Foods property is **2000 Mack Avenue**, not 200. This is a fictional GothTech Studio conversion of the real building. It does not claim an actual tenancy or business relationship.

References checked September 29, 2026:

- O'Connor Real Estate offering brochure, exterior photos, aerial and floor plan: https://www.oconnordetroit.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/2000_Mack_CommOM.pdf
- Google Maps address pin 42.3543515, -83.0392553 and August 2026 Street View: https://www.google.com/maps/search/2000+Mack+Avenue+Detroit/
- Existing OSM footprint way 379485664 from city-data.json; approximately 48.53 x 43.91 m, in the existing map projection.

Reference features: cream block walls, orange plinth, green corrugated roof fascia, black office canopy, small barred windows, overhead loading entrance, roof equipment, yellow bollards, chain-link perimeter, paved yard and parking bays. Building height, yard outline, gates, individual bay positions and grades are interpreted rather than surveyed. The original colorful side mural is represented by new abstract studio artwork, not a copied mural or Google texture. The old generic OSM building and its solid collision volume are replaced.

The existing Serengeti art and GothTech merchandise interiors sit inside the shell. The shared loading entrance connects to both rooms through a clear aisle. Websites open from explicit links in a separate tab. GothTech's public shop currently identifies its catalog as a concept preview; this change does not enable payments or claim products are orderable.

Authoring: Blender 5.2, build_mack_studio.py. Source Mack_GothTech_Studio.blend; runtime public/exports/atwater/mack-gothtech-studio.glb (about 1.5 MB, 17 material meshes). Geometry merged by material; fine facade details hidden at distance and in compact mode. Fences use batched lines, posts and collision proxies. No AI-generation credits used.
