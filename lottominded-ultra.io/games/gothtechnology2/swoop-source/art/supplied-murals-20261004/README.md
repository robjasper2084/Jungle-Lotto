# Five supplied mural adaptations

Tool: built-in image_gen.imagegen, five separate reference edits. Originals and generated PNGs are preserved. Optimized WebP textures are bundled by Vite.

Prompt intent for graffiti 1: full-bleed front-on wall texture, retain original red/silver letters, yellow doodles and original credits, remove flash haze; no new lettering or scenery.
Prompt intent for graffiti 2: same cleanup for its separate wider composition, retain top tags and full artwork.
Prompt intent for graffiti 3: correct photographed tilt and glare while retaining the closer original composition and credits.
Prompt intent for hooded portrait: preserve the navy/red hooded figure and cream scribbled composition, front-on texture with subtle painted plaster grain.
Prompt intent for golden abstract: preserve the yellow/navy/red composition and dark central figure, full square painted texture.

The bridge additions are authored game placements on the five previously unpainted spans. The original eight wall artworks and two painted ceilings remain. All new displays retain their source aspect ratio and clear the riding corridor.
