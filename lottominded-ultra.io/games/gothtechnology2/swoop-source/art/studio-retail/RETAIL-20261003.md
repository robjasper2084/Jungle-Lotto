# GothTech retail and Penny Exchange

The existing GothTechnology catalog is the merchandise source: all 19 names,
images, concept flags and reference prices remain intact. Modeled retail fixtures
and products illustrate that collection; they do not establish sale inventory.

`GothTech_Retail_20261003.blend` is the editable store. The previous store GLB and
master are preserved in `before-retail-polish`. `build_goth_retail.py` builds the
full room in Blender and `optimize_goth_retail.mjs` publishes the browser GLB.
Original approved Higgsfield walnut, cotton, brass, plaster and stone materials
are reused. No new generation credits were spent. Google reference photography
is not embedded in either shop.

`Penny_Exchange.blend` and `LottoMind_Game_Kiosk.blend` model the attached auction
shop and playable 2084 kiosk. Their source and runtime exports are separate from
the pre-existing LottoMind master. The mapped building footprint and open-door
colliders are verified by the pennyShop and lottoStreetEntry tests.

## Hero walking

`../animation-polish/GothTech_Hero_Retail_Walk.blend` contains the original suited
hero with a baked walk retargeted from the installed Kevin Iglesias Human Basic
Motions 2.4 FREE Unity pack. The original skin, mesh, bones and riding animations
are retained. The source FBX remains in that licensed Unity project. The browser
embeds only the baked hero motion in `hero-retail-walk.json` (21 samples / 20 mapped
anatomical bones plus unchanged rig joints). It does not distribute the pack.

`retarget_retail_walk.py` corrects the bind rolls and anatomical rest pose in
Blender. `extract_retail_walk.mjs` converts baked joint rotations into the original
GLB's bind coordinate system; measured bind position drift is below one micron.
`RetailWalker` matches the cycle to speed, blends into idle, retains the captured
hip/spine/arm motion, and uses stance-foot IK to prevent slipping and floor
penetration. The original hero uses it on both gallery and store visits; other
characters retain their existing foot-contact gait.

Official pack: https://marketplace.unity.com/packages/3d/animations/human-basic-motions-free-154271
Author documentation: https://www.keviniglesias.com/documentation/Human%20Basic%20Motions%20FREE.pdf

## Runtime validation

TypeScript check and production packaging succeeded. Six focused physics/walk
tests and ten auction backend tests pass. Browser verification on port 4181
followed the actual Park & enter store flow through walking to arrival, and
confirmed the baked motion provenance on the arrival panel. The auction desk
connected to SQLite, accepted one test bid, reduced free test credits from 20 to
19 and displayed the recorded 1-cent bid. Real bidding and payments remain closed.
Ports 4180 and 4181 share the same handler and serve byte-identical game entries.
