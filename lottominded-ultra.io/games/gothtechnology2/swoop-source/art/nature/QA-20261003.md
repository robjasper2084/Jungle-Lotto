# Local riding, fall and nature update

Updated the Swoop source, shared Digital Static RideCore, actual Elmwood source and the versioned Elmwood snapshot. Preserved existing riders, skins, mapped Tree Tour stops and unrelated work. No commit, push or public deployment was made.

## Changes

- Eye camera banking agrees with steering; eye mode responds sooner to partial steering and side changes, retaining full steering limits and the calm camera option.
- Rebuilt the editable Blender fall controls with floating-point Bezier channels and a bundled free Rigify human metarig reference. Both games use protective arm reach, contact absorption and recovery motion on the existing rider rigs. This is procedural animation, not a downloaded motion-capture clip or physics ragdoll.
- Created original Blender cherry trees, an American robin with wing pivots, and a petal asset using the native Geometry Nodes system and an editable local add-on. Nearby petals use one bounded instanced draw. Reduced motion keeps birds perched and hides falling petals.
- Swoop has seven ornamental cherry sites. Elmwood has nine: three on established lawns, six spaced along the mapped open creek. The tree formerly in the parking area is excluded. Crown clearance excludes parking, lanes, water, buildings and existing placements.
- Recorded CC0 bird, creek, waterfall and large-dog sounds are packaged locally. Water fades by distance and pans with the rider. Creek audio also provides gentle pond ambience; the pond waterfall/fountain zone has a separate recording. Master sound starts enabled in Elmwood and includes nature and dog commands. Pause, menu and mute suppress ambience.

## Limits

This is a local build. Audio playback/decode and pause state were verified, but no claim is made of listening through the user's speakers. Original bird geometry is stylized and anatomically modeled; the field birdsong recording is not claimed to be a robin-only recording. No full-map completion, physical headset/controller test or new performance guarantee was established in this turn. Existing high-detail scene cost and large-bundle build warnings remain.
