# Beach Scene shoreline candidate (November 25, 2026)

Source: **Beach Scene** by BMF, Blend Swap asset 47462 (http://www.blendswap.com/blends/view/47462). The supplied archive's BLENDSWAP_LICENSE.txt declares Creative Commons Zero (Public Domain).

Two geometry derivatives from the supplied, privately exported GLBs are embedded in beachAssetGeometry.ts: Driftwood (99 triangles) and a short grass clump derived from Sea Oat No Seeds (96 triangles). Offline quadric simplification and coordinate quantization are lossy and the game uses its own per-instance color palette; the legacy Blender material colors were not exported. These shapes are not a verified visual reproduction of the original materials or exact local plant species.

Only **high** graphics quality uses the new shapes. Smooth and automatic retain the previous geometries. The existing deterministic beach placement plan, counts, colors, distance fade, water, sand, roads, shadows, collision and three draw-pool structure are unchanged. No new model fetch or external texture is required. The maximum plan caps bound the high-quality candidate to 230 × 96 grass triangles and 30 × 99 driftwood triangles, excluding existing stones.

The large and small seeded grass variants are separately optimized for offline review, not shipped here. Do not merge before matched-angle high/automatic/smooth browser screenshots, beach road/land clearance, and the unchanged native desktop/mobile performance and existing packaging gates pass.
