# Living Birds derivative runtime asset

`living-crow.glb` is adapted from the locally installed **Living Birds**, publisher **Dinopunch**, Unity Asset Store package. The original flyStraight animation was baked in Unity 6000.3.24f1 and prepared in Blender 5.2 for this game's runtime.
