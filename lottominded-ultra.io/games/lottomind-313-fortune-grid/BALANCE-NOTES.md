# Balance Notes

All 24 fictional ventures use a synthetic three-step cost curve unrelated to actual real-estate value. Networks share the same three-venture size. Costs rotate evenly within every network (140/160/180 Detroit Dollars), and base Collaboration Fees rotate evenly (28/35/42).

Unique network bonuses change decision texture rather than declare a district socially superior: preview/control, Hub dividends, rhythm rewards, upgrade efficiency, recurring income, Signal combinations, trade flexibility, and Legacy progress.

The automated suite runs 1,000 seeded Quick 313 matches, rejects deadlocks, impossible phases, non-finite or negative balances, and a broad network-completion imbalance. Standard play still needs longer human balance sessions before Beta approval; CPU personalities use intentionally readable heuristics, not hidden advantages.

Geographic naming and broad directional placement were checked against the City of Detroit's official [Interactive District Map](https://detroitmi.gov/webapp/interactive-district-map) and [Base Unit Tools](https://baseunits.detroitmi.gov/). The City notes that neighborhood boundaries can overlap; Fortune Grid therefore uses respectful, abstract network placement rather than claiming legal boundaries or real-estate ownership.
