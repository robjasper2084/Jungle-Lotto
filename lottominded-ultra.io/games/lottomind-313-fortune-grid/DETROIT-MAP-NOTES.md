# Detroit map placement notes

The selected illustrated overhead map uses a readable clockwise venture circuit over a stylized Detroit composition. Latitude/longitude anchors remain as stop metadata, while the visible route is composed to match the approved board view. It is not intended for navigation or parcel-level representation.

Key placement references:

- Gordie Howe International Bridge: 42.2881, -83.0978, at Delray/West Jefferson. Official project material places the Detroit side along West Jefferson between Green and Campbell Streets: https://gordiehoweinternationalbridge.com/jefferson-barrier-wall-reaches-completion-in-detroit/
- Ambassador Bridge: approximately 42.3124, -83.0743. The two international bridges are intentionally separated on the board rather than grouped as one river landmark.
- Ford Field: 2000 Brush Street, per the venue's official facts page: https://www.fordfield.com/stadium-info/about-ford-field
- Comerica Park: 2100 Woodward Avenue.
- Little Caesars Arena: 2645 Woodward Avenue.
- Michigan Central: the Corktown station campus on Michigan Avenue.
- Guardian Building: Griswold Street in Detroit's Financial District.
- Renaissance Center: Detroit International Riverfront on Jefferson Avenue.

The major radial avenues shown are Woodward, Michigan, Jefferson, Gratiot, and Grand River, with additional anchors for West Vernor, Livernois, Cass, Grand Boulevard, Brush, Russell, Atwater, and Fort.
